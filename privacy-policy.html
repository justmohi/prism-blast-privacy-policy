1. Overview

PRISM BLAST — COMBO BLOCKS is a mobile puzzle game. This Privacy Policy applies to the Game and describes the information practices associated with its features and advertising services.

Important: The Game’s actual behavior depends on the version installed and the services enabled. Please review the sections below, especially advertising, before publishing this policy.

2. Information that may be processed

We do not ask you to create an account to play the Game. Depending on the features and SDKs included in the version you use, the following information may be processed:

  • Gameplay data: score, best score, level progress, settings, and other game state saved on your device.
  • Advertising and device data: advertising identifiers, IP address, device or app information, ad impressions, interactions, and fraud-prevention signals that may be processed by the advertising provider.
  • Diagnostics: technical information such as crash reports or performance data, if diagnostics services are enabled.

We do not intentionally request your contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone recordings for the core puzzle gameplay.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game may use Google AdMob to display ads, including optional rewarded ads that can let you continue a game session after watching an ad. Google and its partners may process data such as device identifiers, IP address, ad delivery and interaction information, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Google’s use of information is governed by Google’s own policies. Learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Ad availability and personalization may depend on your region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law. Where required, the Game or its advertising provider should request consent before serving personalized ads.

4. Local storage and game progress

The Game may save gameplay information such as scores, unlocked levels, progress, and preferences locally on your device so that you can continue playing later. This information is generally available to the Game on that device and may be removed if you clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it.

If a future version adds cloud saves, accounts, analytics, or other services, this policy should be updated to describe those features before release.

5. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or received by service providers when necessary to operate features you use, including Google AdMob for advertising and related measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Those providers may handle information under their own privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Game.

6. Data retention and your choices

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by you, removed by the operating system, or replaced by the Game. Advertising and diagnostic data handled by third-party providers is subject to their retention policies.

  • You can stop using the Game at any time.
  • You may be able to reset local gameplay data by clearing the Game’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can review your Android and Google settings for advertising privacy and personalization options.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information handled by a responsible party.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and audience settings must be configured appropriately for the Game’s intended audience and in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements. If you believe personal information has been handled inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the Game and its locally stored data. However, no storage method or electronic transmission is completely secure. Third-party services maintain their own security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game’s features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Please check this page periodically.
